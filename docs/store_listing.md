## Full description (4000 chars max)
**Avago** is the all-in-one maintenance management app for teams that manage fleets, equipment, and properties.

### Track everything that matters
- Log every service, inspection, and repair across all your assets
- Snap photos, scan receipts, attach documents — all in one place
- Full audit trail with cost tracking and itemized parts & labor

### Work orders, done right
- Create and dispatch work orders to your technicians in seconds
- 7-stage workflow from open → assigned → in progress → complete
- Drag-and-drop dispatch board for busy teams
- Set recurring schedules and never miss a PM again

### Smart inventory
- Track parts and supplies with automatic reorder alerts
- Purchase orders with approval workflows and GRN receiving
- Cycle counts, warehouse movements, multi-location support
- Cost lines on every job — link parts directly to work orders

### Reports that actually help
- 23 pre-built reports: financial, work order, maintenance, and system
- Fixed Asset Register with depreciation (straight-line, DDB, UOP)
- CSV and PDF export for every report
- Date-range presets: this month, last quarter, YTD, and more

### Built for teams
- Real-time chat per work order and per asset
- Role-based permissions — techs see what they need, managers see everything
- Multi-account on one device — personal and work, separate
- Offline-first: every action works without a network connection

### Scout — your AI assistant
- Type or speak a command: "log an oil change on Truck 47"
- Scout fills in your forms automatically
- On-device AI (Gemini Nano on supported devices) for receipts and docs

**Free to start.** Scales with your team.
